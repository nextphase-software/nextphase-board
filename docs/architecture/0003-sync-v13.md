# 0003 — Room sync contract

CONTRACT VERSION: 0003 sync v13 (2026-11-11)

Status: Draft for 0007-W4 v1. Replaces [sync v12](0003-sync-v12.md), whose
document, comment, timer, vote, spotlight, renew, presence and close rules remain
in force word for word. Use the [brief's version set](../briefs/0007-W4-send-ephemeral-workshop-reactions.md).
[Presence v4](0004-presence-v4.md) owns the exact reaction ids, frames, bounds,
budget, refusals and relay order. Joined is unchanged: it gains no reaction field.

Route `react` inside Room.ordered after the existing load, expiry, lock and outer
1,048,576-byte checks and before the edit-only fallback, beside `presence`.
Parse incoming `reacted` separately from document, timer, vote and spotlight
kinds. Use the existing session and socket; no new socket, HTTP route, receipt or
request id.

A reaction is fire-and-forget. The client never queues, retries, resends or
replays a reaction with pending document edits, across a transport attempt, on
reconnect or after an uncertain send; a reaction whose send fails is simply lost.
`refused` with `reaction_too_large`, `bad_reaction` or `reactions_voting` is not
a document refusal, never enters refused-save recovery and never changes presence
or edit state. Over-budget frames are dropped silently by the room.

`reacted` is delivered only to connections admitted when it is sent; a frame
arriving after the receiving session has ended, after a board change or after the
board's voting round started is ignored by the client. Reactions add nothing to
document state, revisions, edited-at, Undo/Redo, history, exports or backups.
